Paste into iPhone Mail

  1. Tap the copy button under the version you want.
  2. Open Settings, then Apps, then Mail, then Signature.
  3. Choose Per Account, tap in the box for eli@ameglobalins.com, delete the old signature, and paste.
  4. If the formatting disappears after pasting, shake the iPhone right away and tap Undo to restore it.
  5. Send yourself a test email to check it.
